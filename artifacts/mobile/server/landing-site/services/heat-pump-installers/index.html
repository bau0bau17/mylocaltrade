Heat Pump Installers

Find local heat pump installers near you

Heat pumps are a low-carbon alternative to gas boilers that can significantly reduce heating bills, particularly with the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant. MyLocalTrade helps you find certified local heat pump installers to assess your home, guide you through grant eligibility and carry out the installation.

What Heat Pump Installers do

  • Air source heat pump (ASHP) installation
  • Ground source heat pump (GSHP) installation
  • Heat pump maintenance and servicing
  • Underfloor heating integration
  • Radiator upgrades for heat pump compatibility
  • Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) grant applications
  • Heat loss surveys and system design

Tips for hiring

  • MCS certification is required for Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant eligibility
  • Get a full heat loss survey before agreeing to any system specification
  • Check whether your home insulation is suitable before installing a heat pump
  • Ask for projections on running costs and expected savings
