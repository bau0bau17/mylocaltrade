# Children-access assessment

**Scope:** MyLocalTrade is an adult property and trades marketplace. Its
features support adults arranging property work, trades services, enquiries,
quotes and related service arrangements. It has no child-directed features,
child accounts, child content or intentional targeting of children.

Children may encounter or access the service incidentally (for example through
general web/app access), but the product is not designed or marketed to them.
The proportionate control is a clear, required registration declaration that
the person is **18 or older and legally capable of entering the relevant
service arrangements**, for both customers and traders. The API validates the
declaration and records its boolean value and timestamp with the account.

The service does not collect date of birth, perform age assurance or introduce
age verification. Existing identity and trader verification processes are
unchanged. This control is reviewed periodically and also whenever product
scope, audience, features or service arrangements change.

**Manual owner note:** The legal/product owner should re-evaluate this
assessment and controls if the product scope or audience changes.
