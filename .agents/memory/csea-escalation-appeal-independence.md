---
name: CSEA escalation and appeal independence
description: Safety escalation must proceed independently of ordinary appeal status while keeping evidence restricted.
---

An open ordinary appeal must never delay or block a specialist CSEA escalation. Once escalated, ordinary admins lose access to the restricted case and its evidence, while the specialist investigation itself holds conversation media until it is completed.

**Why:** Appeal handling is an ordinary moderation process; suspected child sexual exploitation content requires prompt specialist handling. Coupling them risks delaying a safeguarding action or losing evidence through account cleanup.

**How to apply:** Keep CSEA escalation, restricted queues, transcript access, and appeal administration separately authorized. Any CSEA state transition must preserve the evidence hold without exposing paths or media to ordinary administrators.
