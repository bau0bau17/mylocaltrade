---
name: Public policy language
description: User-facing policy copy must stay neutral, accurate, and aligned across native and public landing surfaces.
---

Do not expose internal workflow roles or states in public policy copy. Native and landing legal/help text may be shorter on mobile, but must preserve the same legal meaning, operator identity, contact route, and current launch-stage commitments.

**Why:** Terms such as “Admin review” and vague company identity language are internal or incomplete, and mismatched rendered HTML and hydrated landing copy can show conflicting policy text.

**How to apply:** Use neutral “MyLocalTrade review” language. Keep public landing HTML and its matching hydrated bundle synchronized. Preserve the Apple-only subscription, 18+, conditional OTP, marketplace, and cookie/tracking positions unless product behavior changes.
