OWNER REASSIGNMENT

The company owner may reassign an active job to:

- themselves; or
- any active Employee belonging to the same company.

Employees cannot reassign jobs.

Allow reassignment after quote/hire and during an active job, while preserving the existing lifecycle.

Do not allow reassignment to:

- a pending invite;
- a removed/deactivated member;
- another company’s member;
- an arbitrary user ID;
- the same currently assigned person;
- a completed or cancelled job.

Reassignment must not:

- create or duplicate a quote;
- change the original quote author;
- reset hire, booking, appointment or completion state;
- delete messages or audit history;
- create a new conversation;
- expose personal profile photos outside the existing privacy rules.

PERMISSIONS AFTER REASSIGNMENT

After reassignment:

- the new assignee immediately gains the existing assigned-person permissions;
- the previous assignee immediately becomes read-only;
- the owner retains owner visibility;
- the conversation header updates to the new assignee;
- the company name remains visible;
- existing conversation and job history remains unchanged.

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

Create one clear customer-visible system message, for example:

“Your job is now being handled by [First name] from [Company name].”

Do not expose internal role, membership or user IDs.

Send:

- one customer notification;
- one notification to the new assignee;
- an internal notification to the previous assignee when they remain an active member;
- owner notification only where required, with recipient deduplication.

Do not create duplicate system messages or notifications during retries.

MEMBER REMOVAL / DEACTIVATION

When an employee is removed or deactivated:

- all active/open jobs currently assigned to that employee must be reassigned automatically to the owner;
- completed and cancelled jobs retain the historical assigned person;
- historical message/quote/action authorship must remain unchanged;
- the removed employee loses access immediately;
- job handover and membership removal must not leave an active job assigned to an inactive member.

Define “active/open” using the existing canonical job states rather than duplicating status strings.

For customer-visible active jobs, create the appropriate single reassignment system message and notification.

The removal flow must be atomic or otherwise designed so partial handover cannot leave inconsistent permissions.

RACE SAFETY

Review and test races between:

- owner reassignment and an outgoing message;
- owner reassignment and quote submission/revision;
- member removal and an outgoing message;
- member removal and booking/completion actions;
- two simultaneous reassignment requests;
- reassignment retries.

Expected rule:

- whichever transaction commits first determines the valid state;
- no action from a non-assigned or removed member may persist after reassignment/removal has taken effect;
- no duplicate message, quote, audit event or notification may be created.
