--------------------------------------------------
PRODUCT INTENT
--------------------------------------------------

I want to support the following model:

- A business/company has one shared business profile.
- Each authorised person working for that business has their own individual user account.
- Each individual can have their own personal profile photo/headshot.
- The business can have its own logo.
- Owner/director/sole trader can manage the business logo.
- Employees cannot change the business logo.
- Employees must be verified and approved before acting on behalf of the company.
- In a future phase, the first authorised person who sends a valid quote or explicitly takes the lead becomes the assigned representative for that job.
- The customer should then see both:
  - the business identity
  - the individual person handling the job

Example:

Business:
ABC Electrical LTD

Assigned representative:
John Smith
[John’s profile photo]

--------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 — SAFE PRE-LAUNCH SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------

Please assess whether the following Phase 1 can be implemented safely before launch:

1. Individual profile photo/headshot for trader users.
2. New onboarding role:
   - Employee
3. Mandatory employee authorisation/employment letter.
4. Safe company association approval.
5. Business logo editable only by:
   - Owner
   - Director
   - Sole trader for their own business
6. Employees can edit only their own personal profile details/photo.
7. No full team inbox, job assignment or reassignment yet.
8. No RevenueCat/IAP entitlement changes yet.

Please confirm whether this Phase 1 is genuinely small enough and safe enough before launch.

--------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 — DEFERRED UNTIL AFTER LAUNCH
--------------------------------------------------

Please assess, but do not implement:

- Multiple active company members under one business.
- Shared company lead visibility.
- First person to send a valid quote becomes assigned representative.
- Server-side concurrency protection for assignment.
- Individual name/photo shown in quote, conversation and job.
- Post-assignment notifications to the assigned representative.
- Owner/director oversight.
- Explicit reassignment.
- Employee offboarding.
- Historical job attribution.
- Shared business subscription entitlement.

EMPLOYEE AUTHORISATION LETTER
--------------------------------------------------

For an Employee account, the required letter should confirm:

- employee full name
- business/company name
- that they are employed by or authorised to represent the company
- that they may communicate with customers and provide quotes on behalf of the company
- name/title of the authorising representative
- date
- signature or appropriate company confirmation

This document should be additional to existing required verification documents.

Please assess:
- where this document type should live
- how admin reviews it
- whether a new document type is sufficient
- whether the employee should remain pending until approval
