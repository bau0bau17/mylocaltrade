Goal

- MyLocalTrade manages Premium feature access.
- Apple manages subscriptions.
- Apple manages billing.
- Apple manages renewals.
- Apple manages cancellations.
- Apple manages refunds.

This simplifies the legal side, avoids conflicting information, and keeps the UX consistent with App Store expectations.
