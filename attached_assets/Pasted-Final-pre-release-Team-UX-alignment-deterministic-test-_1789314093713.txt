1. OTHER EMPLOYEE'S CLAIMED JOB — SAFE PLACEHOLDER
==================================================

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

A conversation claimed by another employee is currently omitted completely from the employee's Messages list.

SECURITY:
This is safe.

UX REQUIREMENT:
The intended product behavior is for team employees to know that a company job has already been claimed by a colleague, without seeing any protected customer/conversation content.

For an ACTIVE team employee, show a minimal safe placeholder entry for a conversation assigned to another employee.

Example:

Claimed by Lucian

or:

Assigned to Lucian

Nothing more sensitive should be exposed.

The placeholder must NOT reveal:

- customer name
- customer initials/avatar if derived from customer
- phone/email
- enquiry text
- service description unless explicitly proven safe and desired
- message preview
- photos
- quote
- appointment
- contact details
- last message
- signed media
- moderation/report information

Use only the minimum identifiers needed to render a stable placeholder plus the assignee's permitted display name.

The placeholder must NOT be openable as a conversation.

Tapping it should either do nothing or show a simple informational message such as:

“This job is being handled by Lucian.”

Do not allow navigation to /messages/:id from this placeholder.

DIRECT API SECURITY MUST REMAIN:

No protected content returned.

Owner:
continues to receive/open the full conversation.

continues to receive/open the full conversation.
