CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

The moderation/reporting system currently enforces a 30-day period in which an eligible user may appeal a resolved moderation decision.

The evidence-retention system also relies on this 30-day appeal window.

However, current user-facing wording does not explicitly disclose the 30-day appeal deadline.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The existing Complaints wording already mentions 30 days for a substantive complaint response.

That is NOT the same thing as the moderation appeal deadline.

Do not merge or confuse these two different 30-day concepts.

Determine whether the moderation appeal deadline is already disclosed anywhere.

IF THE GAP EXISTS

Add concise, accurate wording explaining:

- an eligible moderation decision can be appealed;
- the appeal must be submitted within 30 days of the moderation decision;
- after that period, the appeal option may no longer be available;
- submitting an appeal does not automatically reverse the original decision;
- MyLocalTrade reviews appeals separately from the original moderation decision;
- safety escalation, including restricted specialist handling where applicable, may continue independently of an ordinary appeal.

Do NOT publicly mention CSEA operational details, specialist email allowlists, internal queues, or restricted evidence-handling implementation.

Use neutral user-facing wording.

  “Appeal available until [date]”
  rather than requiring the user to calculate 30 days themselves.

- do not show an active Appeal action;
- use clear wording such as “Appeal period ended” if useful.
