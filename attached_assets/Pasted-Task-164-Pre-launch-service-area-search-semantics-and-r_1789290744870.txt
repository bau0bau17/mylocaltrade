CURRENT FINDING

Search currently applies radius using the trader’s verified business postcode / geocoded base location.

A declared service area does NOT currently override an out-of-radius business base.

Example:

Trader business base:
London

Trader declares that they serve:
Milton Keynes

Customer search:
Boiler
Milton Keynes
20 miles

Current result:
Trader is excluded because the business base is outside the 20-mile radius.

This may not match the intended MyLocalTrade marketplace model.

2. DEFINE CORRECT SEARCH SEMANTICS
==================================================

For a trades marketplace, distinguish:

BUSINESS BASE
Where the trader/business is located.

SERVICE AREA
Where the trader is willing to accept jobs.

A customer searching in Milton Keynes should normally be able to find a trader who has explicitly configured Milton Keynes as a valid service area, even if the trader's business base is outside the customer's selected radius.

However:
do NOT make every trader nationwide.
do NOT infer service areas from profile text.
do NOT fabricate geographic coverage.

Use only authoritative trader-configured service coverage.

Preferred rule, if supported safely by the existing data model:

A trader qualifies geographically if EITHER:

A. their verified business/base location falls within the selected customer radius;

OR

B. the customer's searched location falls within a service area explicitly configured by that trader.

The selected customer radius should still apply normally to base-location matches.

An explicit configured service-area match is a separate positive coverage signal and should not be rejected merely because the trader's office/base is outside that radius.

If this interpretation conflicts with the existing product model, report that before implementing.
