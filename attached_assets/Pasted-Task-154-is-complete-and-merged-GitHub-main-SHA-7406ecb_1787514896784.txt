What changed
Notification taps now route these conversation-scoped events to the existing /messages/:id screen:
new messages
quotes received, revised, withdrawn, accepted, declined
bookings proposed, confirmed, cancelled, reminded
job reassignment
job completion / work marked complete / review invite

subscription updates → Billing
unknown/report notifications → app root, with no speculative navigation.
