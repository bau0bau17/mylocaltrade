==================================================
A. TEAM CONVERSATION PRIVACY / CLAIM OWNERSHIP
==================================================

CURRENT REAL DEVICE BEHAVIOUR

A company owner can invite employees/team members.

When one employee claims/handles a job, other employees in the same company can currently:

- see the conversation in Messages;
- open it;
- read messages;
- see customer/job information;
- see appointment information and other conversation details.

The UI already indicates things such as:

“CLAIMED · LUCIAN”

and inside the thread:

“Lucian Sabău is handling this job — it’s read-only for you.”

Read-only is NOT sufficient.

A non-assigned employee should not be able to read another employee’s customer conversation merely because they belong to the same company.


==================================================
1. REQUIRED ROLE MODEL
==================================================

BUSINESS OWNER / MAIN TRADER ACCOUNT

The company owner may:

- see all company conversations;
- open all company conversations;
- supervise jobs handled by employees;
- manage Team members;
- manage subscription/billing.

This owner access is intentional.


TEAM EMPLOYEE

For a conversation claimed/assigned to THAT employee:

- full authorized conversation access;
- messages;
- enquiry context;
- photos;
- quotes;
- appointment information;
- customer contact details where existing job rules permit them;
- normal job workflow.

For a conversation claimed/assigned to ANOTHER employee:

The employee must NOT receive or view the conversation content.

In the Messages list, show only a minimal safe state such as:

“Claimed by Lucian”

or:

“Assigned to Lucian”

Do not expose unnecessary additional information.

In particular, for a conversation claimed by somebody else, audit and prevent leakage of:

- customer name;
- customer email;
- customer phone number;
- service/job description;
- job reference if not necessary;
- last-message preview;
- conversation messages;
- images;
- enquiry photos;
- quotes;
- appointment details;
- contact details;
- report/moderation data;
- signed media URLs.

The user specifically wants the employee-facing card to communicate who claimed the job, without exposing the other employee’s conversation content.

B. TEAM SUBSCRIPTION DOWNGRADE → EMPLOYEE ACCESS
==================================================

PRODUCT RULE

Team plans provide employee seats.

Solo plans:

Premium Monthly £9.99
Premium Annual

do NOT provide employee seats.

If the OWNER changes from a Team subscription to a Solo subscription, Team employees must no longer retain company access once the Solo subscription becomes EFFECTIVE.


==================================================
6. VERY IMPORTANT — APPLE DEFERRED DOWNGRADE
==================================================

Preserve the subscription lifecycle fix already implemented.

Example:

Owner currently has:
Team 5 Annual

Owner chooses:
Premium Monthly £9.99

Apple schedules the downgrade for the next renewal.

BEFORE renewal:

Team 5 remains the effective product.
The paid Team period is still active.
Employees KEEP their Team access and seats.

Do NOT restrict them early merely because PRODUCT_CHANGE or a scheduled downgrade exists.


AFTER Apple/RevenueCat confirms Premium Monthly is the EFFECTIVE active product:

Team seat allowance becomes 0.

At that point:

- Team employees lose company/team operational access automatically;
- owner remains able to use their own trader account;
- owner keeps Billing & Plan access;
- owner can upgrade back to an eligible Team plan.

RevenueCat/server-effective entitlement is authoritative.

Do NOT use a locally selected future product to restrict employees early.


==================================================
7. DO NOT DELETE TEAM MEMBERS JUST BECAUSE PLAN CHANGES
==================================================

Preferred behaviour:

Do NOT permanently delete existing Team memberships when the owner moves to Solo.

Instead make them subscription-restricted/inactive for Team access.

Reason:

If the owner later upgrades back to an eligible Team plan, existing membership relationships can be restored according to the available seat allowance rather than requiring every employee to be recreated.

Audit whether the current schema already supports this through entitlement/seat gating.

Use the smallest safe implementation.

Do not introduce a major new membership-state architecture unless necessary.


==================================================
8. EMPLOYEE EXPERIENCE WHEN TEAM PLAN IS NO LONGER ACTIVE
==================================================

Employees should still be able to authenticate enough to understand why access is unavailable.

Do NOT leave them inside the company dashboard with broken/403 screens everywhere.

Show a clear restricted-access state such as:

“Team access unavailable”

“This company no longer has an active Team subscription.”

“Please contact the business account owner to restore Team access.”

Where safely available to an existing authorized Team member, show the server-authoritative owner contact, for example:

“Account owner: owner@example.com”

Do not hardcode an email address.

Do not expose an email to people who are not members of that company.

Employees should NOT be shown subscription purchase/change controls intended for the owner.


OWNER EXPERIENCE

The business owner must NOT be locked out by the Team-plan restriction.

Owner retains access to:

- their own trader account/profile;
- Billing & Plan;
- subscription management;
- Team management as appropriate;
- legal/support/account settings.

This is essential so the owner can upgrade back to a Team plan.
