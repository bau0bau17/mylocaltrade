2. Downgrade and expiry behaviour

Never delete employees, memberships, jobs, quotes, conversations or historical attribution because a subscription expires or is downgraded.

If the new employee allowance is lower than current active employees plus pending invitations:

- block new invitations immediately;
- prevent accepting an invitation when no seat is available;
- release seats when invitations expire or are cancelled;
- suspend only the employees exceeding the paid allowance;
- suspended employees become read-only and cannot claim, message, quote, book, edit or otherwise act for the company;
- preserve all historical names, photos, messages, quotes and job attribution;
- allow the owner to select which employees remain active;
- make suspension and reactivation reversible;
- never count the owner as an employee seat;
- keep an absolute maximum of 20 employees.

If deterministic temporary selection is necessary during a downgrade, preserve the longest-standing active memberships and suspend the newest ones until the owner changes the selection. Document and test this rule.

3. Owner seat-management UI

- current plan;
- employee allowance;
- active employee seats;
- reserved pending-invite seats;
- available seats;
- suspended employees;
- clear explanation when the business is over capacity.

The owner must be able to activate or suspend employees within the paid allowance. All changes must be transactional and race-safe.

Employees must never see billing details, product identifiers, prices, subscription-management controls or seat-selection controls.

- retain read-only access to appropriate historical company work;
- be unable to perform any company action;
- never see a purchase or restore-purchases prompt.

A removed/revoked employee must still lose company access immediately, including existing sessions.
