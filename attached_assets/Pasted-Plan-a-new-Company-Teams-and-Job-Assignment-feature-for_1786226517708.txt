Plan a new “Company Teams and Job Assignment” feature for MyLocalTrade.

PLANNING ONLY

Do not edit code, create migrations, commit or push anything yet.

First inspect the existing database schema, authentication, trader accounts, business profiles, verification, quotes, conversations, notifications, subscriptions and audit logs.

CURRENT ARCHITECTURE FINDING

A trader business currently has one login only. The Owner/Director/Employee onboarding field describes the person completing verification but does not create actual company roles or separate employee accounts.

PRODUCT GOAL

A business owner must be able to invite employees, with each employee having:

- Their own login
- Their own name and personal profile photo
- Membership of the owner’s company
- Access determined by their company role
- Their own identity recorded when taking actions

The company must retain one shared:

- Business profile
- Business logo
- Public trader profile
- Subscription
- Leads/jobs workspace
- Customer-facing company identity

CORE REQUIREMENTS TO PLAN

1. Company and membership model

- Existing trader businesses should become companies without breaking current accounts.
- The existing trader should become the company owner.
- Support at minimum Owner and Employee roles.
- Explain whether an Admin/Manager role should be included now or later.
- A user must not silently belong to multiple companies unless deliberately supported.

2. Invitations and account access

- Owner can invite an employee securely.
- Define invite creation, acceptance, expiry, cancellation and resend behaviour.
- Define what happens if the invited email already has a MyLocalTrade account.
- Owner can remove or deactivate a member.
- Removed members must immediately lose company access.

3. Permissions

- Only Owner can edit the company logo.
- Only Owner can manage members and company-sensitive settings.
- Specify who can edit the business profile, services, availability and subscription.
- Enforce permissions server-side, not only by hiding mobile controls.
- Existing single-account traders must continue working normally.

4. Leads, quotes and job claiming

- Eligible company members may see shared company leads.
- The first member who submits the company quote becomes the assigned person for that job.
- Quote claiming must be atomic to prevent two employees from claiming simultaneously.
- Only one company quote should exist for the same enquiry/conversation.
- Other members should see “Quote submitted by [name]” or “Job claimed by [name]”.
- Define whether the owner can reassign a job and how reassignment is recorded.
- Define what happens if the assigned employee is removed or deactivated.

5. Customer-facing identity

- Search cards and public profiles continue showing the company logo.
- Conversation headers show the assigned person’s name and personal profile photo.
- The company name should remain visible so the customer understands which business the person represents.
- Personal photos must follow the existing conversation privacy rules.
- Define the customer experience before a company member has claimed the job.

6. Job lifecycle

Preserve assignment consistently through:

- Quote
- Hire
- Appointment
- In-progress work
- Completion
- Review
- Dispute/admin inspection

Explain which actions may be performed by the assigned employee, owner and other company members.

7. Notifications and visibility

- Define which members receive new-lead notifications.
- After a job is claimed, define which notifications go to the assigned person, owner and other employees.
- Avoid duplicate notifications to the customer.
- Define whether other company members can read and/or participate in the conversation.

8. Audit and security

Record human-readable audit events for:

- Member invited/accepted/removed
- Role changed
- Job claimed/reassigned
- Quote submitted
- Sensitive company-setting changes

Keep detailed metadata internally without exposing raw payloads in the normal Admin audit-log UI.

9. Subscription and limits

- Determine whether employees are included in the existing company subscription.
- Recommend whether member/seat limits are needed now.
- Do not change billing behaviour without explicitly identifying the impact.

10. Migration and compatibility

- Provide a safe migration path for all existing trader accounts.
- Avoid breaking existing quotes, conversations, jobs, reviews or subscriptions.
- Explain rollback considerations.
- Identify any production backend deployment, database migration, mobile build or Admin deployment required.
