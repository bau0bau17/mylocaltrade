This is a pre-launch P0 subscription-state refresh fix plus a release-gap audit. Treat it as a continuation of the existing Company Teams billing work, not as a new feature expansion.

CURRENT CONFIRMED DEFECT

After the owner successfully purchases or changes to a Team subscription, the Team screen does not automatically refresh its employee-seat allowance. It continues showing the previous allowance until the user manually pulls down to refresh.

For example, after purchasing or changing the Team plan, the correct allowance must appear automatically without reopening the screen, signing out, or using pull-to-refresh.

REQUIRED BEHAVIOUR

Inspect the real RevenueCat purchase, restore, subscription-sync, Billing, Pricing, Team context and Team list flows before editing anything.

After any successful:

- Team 5 / Team 10 / Team 20 purchase;
- upgrade or downgrade;
- Restore purchases;
- return to the app after managing the subscription in the App Store;

the application must:

1. Wait for RevenueCat to confirm success.
2. Call the existing backend subscription-sync flow.
3. Invalidate and refetch every relevant server-backed query, including:
   - subscription status;
   - company team-context;
   - Team member list;
   - employee-seat allowance and usage.
4. Update the Team screen automatically, even if that screen was already mounted.
5. Use a short, bounded retry only if RevenueCat/backend propagation is temporarily delayed.
6. Show a clear “Updating your plan…” state while synchronization is happening.
7. If synchronization cannot be confirmed, keep the previous server-authorized allowance and show a retry action.

SECURITY AND DATA RULES

- The server remains the only authority for the product identifier, effective Team tier and seat allowance.
- Never grant or calculate employee seats only on the client.
- Never infer Team 10 merely from the current member count or the presence of an employee.
- A Solo Monthly or Solo Yearly subscription must always provide zero employee seats once Team billing enforcement is enabled.
- Pending invitations count only according to the existing server rules.
- Cached subscription/team state must be isolated by authenticated user so switching accounts on the same device cannot expose the previous owner’s allowance.
- Employees must remain unable to purchase, restore, manage billing or see owner subscription controls.
