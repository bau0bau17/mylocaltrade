One clarification after reviewing the trader account:

The email/phone currently shown in Enquiries & Leads is correct for the examples I tested because those customers have already hired the trader, so please KEEP the existing post-hire contact visibility rules unchanged.

However, I noticed that “Enquiries & Leads” and “Messages” currently feel quite similar from a trader UX perspective.

Please do a small targeted review of these two screens and make their purpose clearly distinct without merging them or redesigning the app.

Expected distinction:

ENQUIRIES & LEADS
This should act as the trader's lead/job overview.

It should focus on:
- customer name
- requested service
- short enquiry/job description
- date
- current lead/job status
- Job Reference after hire, where available
- contact details only when the existing post-hire rules allow them
- tapping the item can continue into the relevant job/conversation

Relevant statuses may include the existing equivalents of:
- New
- Responded
- Quoted
- Hired
- Completed

Please reuse the statuses already implemented rather than creating a new workflow unnecessarily.

MESSAGES
This should remain the communication inbox.

It should focus on:
- customer name
- service/job title
- Job Reference where available
- most recent message/event preview
- unread/read state if already supported
- current high-level job status
- tapping opens the conversation directly

For example, instead of two rows that both only show:

Lucian Sabau
The customer confirmed the job is done...

please make them easier to distinguish, such as:

Lucian Sabau
Boiler · MLT-000008
[latest message]

and

Lucian Sabau
Electrical issue · MLT-000007
[latest message]

Do not expose contact details in Messages before hire.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not merge Enquiries & Leads with Messages.
- Do not create another messaging system.
- Do not change quote, hire, booking, completion or contact-visibility logic.
- Keep this a small UX clarification.
- Reuse existing data.
- No broad audit.
- No EAS build.
- No deploy/publish.
- Minimum relevant checks only.

Afterwards please confirm:
1. Whether the two screens were already backed by different data/logic.
