Pre-launch P0 audit/fix: verify Team subscription products, RevenueCat mapping and employee-seat entitlement end to end.

This comes directly from the latest release audit.

CURRENT RISK

The release audit found that the API only grants Team employee seats when a purchased Team product is recognised by the configured product-to-seat mapping.

If a Team subscription is active in RevenueCat but the product ID is absent or mismatched in the deployed Team seat map, the account can become Premium-active while receiving zero employee seats.

That must not be possible for a paid Team customer.

Inspect all currently implemented Team products/packages and determine the exact intended seat count for each.

- Team 5
- Team 10
- Team 20

Verify the exact product IDs/package IDs already used by the current app. Do not invent or rename IDs.

CHECK END TO END

1. Mobile subscription products/packages
2. RevenueCat package/product identifiers referenced by the app
3. RevenueCat entitlement used for Premium/Team access
4. API/server Team product recognition
5. TEAM_PRODUCT_SEAT_MAP or equivalent deployed configuration
6. Employee seat count granted for each Team tier
7. Solo Premium versus Team Premium behaviour
8. Subscription restore
9. Upgrade/downgrade between Team tiers
10. Team subscription cancellation while entitlement remains active until expiry
11. Expiry/downgrade back to the correct non-Team state

EXPECTED RESULT

A paid recognised Team subscription must deterministically grant the correct number of employee seats.

For example, conceptually:

Team 5 → 5 employee seats
Team 10 → 10 employee seats
Team 20 → 20 employee seats

Use the actual existing business definition in the repository if those counts differ.

There must be no valid paid Team state where:
- Premium is active
- Team plan is recognised
- employee seat allowance is zero because of a product-ID/config mismatch
