ASSIGNMENT RULE

For a new company lead, the conversation initially has no assigned person.

The first active company member who successfully performs a customer-facing action claims the job:

- sends the first outbound text message;
- sends the first outbound photo/message attachment; or
- submits the company quote.

The following must never claim a job:

- viewing/opening the lead;
- reading messages;
- loading photos;
- drafting text;
- receiving a customer message;
- system-generated messages;
- background refresh or notification handling.

ATOMIC CLAIMING

Claiming and the customer-facing action must be one atomic operation.

If two members act simultaneously:

- exactly one member becomes assigned;
- only the winning member’s message or quote is created;
- the losing action must fail before becoming visible to the customer;
- the losing member receives a clear “This job was claimed by [name]” response;
- no duplicate quote, message, system event or customer notification may be created.

Reuse the Phase 0 assignment columns and existing database locking/constraints.

ACCESS AFTER CLAIMING

Assigned member:

- can message, quote and perform the existing permitted job actions.

Other employees:

- may read the complete company job/conversation;
- see “Job claimed by [name]” or “Quote submitted by [name]”;
- cannot message, quote, upload chat attachments, book, complete or otherwise change the job;
- must fail server-side even if they bypass the mobile UI.

Owner:

- may read every company job.
- If the owner originally claims the job, the owner is the assigned person.
- Do not implement manual reassignment yet; that belongs to Phase 3.

CUSTOMER-FACING IDENTITY

Before assignment:

- display the company name and company logo;
- use the existing fallback when no logo exists.

After assignment:

- display the assigned person’s name and personal profile photo;
- keep the company name clearly visible;
- never replace the person’s photo with the company logo;
- preserve existing profile-photo privacy rules.

Existing single-login conversations already assigned to their owner must continue displaying exactly as expected.

COMPANY MEMBER UI

For unclaimed leads:

- active members can open and inspect the lead;
- clearly show that it is available/unclaimed.

For claimed jobs:

- show who claimed it;
- other employees get a clearly read-only experience;
- disable or hide customer-facing action controls for non-assigned employees;
- server permissions remain authoritative.

NOTIFICATIONS

- New unclaimed lead: notify all active company members.
- After assignment: send customer/job-action notifications to the assigned member and the owner.
- If the assigned member is the owner, send only one notification.
- Other employees should not continue receiving routine notifications for that claimed job.
- Never generate duplicate customer notifications or duplicate system messages.

AUDIT

Add human-readable audit events for:

- JOB CLAIMED
- COMPANY QUOTE SUBMITTED / existing equivalent with acting member identity

Store acting user, company, conversation and relevant metadata internally without exposing raw payloads in the normal Admin audit-log UI.

- removed members remain locked out;
