Approved with the recommended defaults, with the following clarifications and implementation controls.

PRODUCT DECISIONS

1. A user may belong to only one company in v1: approved.

2. Invitations are limited to new email addresses in v1: approved. Existing customer-account conversion can be designed separately later.

3. Roles: Owner and Employee only. Defer Manager.

4. Business profile, services, working hours, logo, subscription and team management remain owner-only.

5. Employees may read jobs claimed by colleagues but cannot message, quote, book, complete or otherwise act on those jobs unless the owner reassigns the job to them.

6. Review replies remain owner-only.

7. New-lead notifications go to all active company members. Do not create duplicate customer-facing notifications.

8. The owner may reassign a job after hire. Reassignment must:
- be recorded in the audit log;
- update the assigned person consistently;
- create a clear system message visible to the customer;
- update the conversation header to the newly assigned person;
- not reset or duplicate the existing quote, booking or job state.

9. When a member is removed or deactivated, their active/open jobs are reassigned to the owner automatically. Record this in the audit log and preserve the complete conversation and job history.

10. Defer “work done by [first name]” on public reviews.

11. Use a maximum of 10 active members per company in v1, with no billing or RevenueCat changes. Please keep this limit configurable rather than scattering a hard-coded value throughout the codebase.

JOB CLAIMING CLARIFICATION

A job must be claimed atomically by the first company member who performs a customer-facing action:

- sends the first outbound message; or
- submits the company quote.

Merely viewing or opening a lead must not claim it.

After the job is claimed:

- the assigned member may continue acting;
- other employees have read-only access;
- the owner may reassign it;
- another employee must not be able to submit a duplicate quote or send customer-facing messages unless reassigned.

The customer should see:

- the company logo and company name before assignment;
- the assigned person’s name and personal profile photo, together with the company name, after assignment.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTROL

Do not implement all four phases in one uninterrupted task.

Start with the first Foundation/Phase 0 implementation phase only:

- additive, backward-compatible database foundations;
- membership/permission helper;
- feature flag defaulting to OFF;
- migration/backfill design for existing single-login traders;
- refactoring only the permission checks included in this phase;
- tests proving existing users and flows remain unchanged while the flag is OFF.

The migration must be additive and safe for the currently deployed mobile version. Turning the feature flag off should disable the new behaviour, although the additive database structures may remain.

After completing this first phase:

- run the complete relevant test suites;
- perform a focused security review of the membership helper;
- commit and push to origin/main;
- report the commit hash, migrations, files changed and checks run;
- explain the rollback procedure;
- confirm that the current production app remains compatible;
- STOP and wait for approval before implementing invitations, team UI or shared-job behaviour.

Do not deploy or republish production automatically.
