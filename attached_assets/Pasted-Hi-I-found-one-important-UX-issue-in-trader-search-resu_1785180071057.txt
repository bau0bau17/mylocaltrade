Hi,

I found one important UX issue in trader search results that I want to fix before launch.

I have attached screenshots showing the current behaviour.

CURRENT ISSUE

The customer is searching in:

Milton Keynes

The trader correctly appears because Milton Keynes is included in the trader’s Service Areas.

However, the trader card displays:

London

which is the trader’s business location/address area.

Technically the search result is correct, but from the customer’s perspective this is confusing.

A customer searching in Milton Keynes may think:

“Why am I seeing a trader from London?”

--------------------------------------------------
EXPECTED BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------

On trader cards shown in location-based customer results, please prioritise the relevant matching SERVICE AREA rather than the trader’s business address/location.

Example:

Customer search location:
Milton Keynes

Trader:
Business address/location = London
Service Areas = Milton Keynes, Northampton

Expected trader card:

📍 Serves Milton Keynes

or:

📍 Milton Keynes service area

Preferred wording:

“Serves Milton Keynes”

This makes it immediately clear why the trader appears in the search results.

--------------------------------------------------
WHERE THIS SHOULD APPLY
--------------------------------------------------

Please inspect the trader cards used in:

- Home → Featured Traders in [customer location]
- Search results
- Traders screen where results are location-aware
- any other customer-facing trader list that is filtered based on Service Areas

Where the card is being shown because of a specific customer/search location, show the matching service area.

Example:

Customer/search location:
Northampton

Trader service areas:
Milton Keynes
Northampton

Card should show:

📍 Serves Northampton

not:

📍 London

--------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS ADDRESS MUST REMAIN SEPARATE
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT change the trader’s stored business address.

Do NOT overwrite existing profile data.

Do NOT expose the trader’s full/private business address.

This is only a customer-facing display improvement on trader cards.

The business address/location can remain available wherever the existing product rules intentionally show it, such as the appropriate trader profile/business-information context.

--------------------------------------------------
FALLBACK BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------

Please use sensible fallback logic.

Preferred order:

1. If the trader card is being displayed for a specific searched/customer location and that location matches one of the trader’s Service Areas:
   → show “Serves [matching area]”

2. If there is no specific search location but an appropriate Service Area can be shown:
   → show a useful Service Area value if consistent with the current screen.

3. Only fall back to the existing trader/business location where there is no meaningful Service Area context.

Do not fabricate a matching area.

Use the actual trader Service Areas and the same location-normalisation logic already used by search.

--------------------------------------------------

Trader:
