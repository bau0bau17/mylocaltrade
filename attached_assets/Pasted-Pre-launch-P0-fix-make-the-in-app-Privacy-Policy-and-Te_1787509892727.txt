Pre-launch P0 fix: make the in-app Privacy Policy and Terms accurately match the current production behaviour.

This task comes directly from the latest read-only release audit.

Please keep this tightly scoped to legal/disclosure accuracy. Inspect the actual current implementation first, then update only the relevant Privacy Policy / Terms copy and any directly related legal-display logic.

Do not change product behaviour just to match old legal text. The legal text must be corrected to describe what the app actually does.


ISSUE 1 — PHONE VERIFICATION / RCS / SMS COPY IS INACCURATE

The current Privacy Policy / Terms describe RCS as trader-only and customer verification as SMS-only.

The current implementation uses Twilio Verify and can use Twilio Verify RCS Upgrade with SMS fallback.

Customer phone verification also uses the Twilio Verify path.

Therefore the legal wording must not make a false statement that:

- RCS is exclusively trader-only
or
- customer verification is always SMS-only

Please inspect the actual current verification flows and write platform/provider-neutral, accurate wording.

Preferred approach:

Explain that MyLocalTrade may send one-time verification/security codes using supported messaging channels such as SMS or RCS where available, with fallback depending on device, network and provider support.

Make it clear that:
- these messages are for account access, verification, security and trust purposes
- they are not marketing/promotional messages
- delivery channel may vary
- Twilio or another authorised communications provider may process the delivery

Do not promise that RCS is available when provider-side activation is not yet confirmed.


ISSUE 2 — SUBSCRIPTION / BILLING COPY IS TOO APPLE-ONLY

The current app supports RevenueCat subscription infrastructure and has configuration for iOS and Android.

Privacy / Terms currently describe subscription billing, cancellation and refunds in Apple/iOS-only terms.

Please correct this so the legal copy remains accurate across supported app-store platforms.

Use wording such as:

- subscriptions are purchased and managed through the app store/platform through which the subscription was purchased
- Apple App Store subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple
- Google Play subscriptions, when offered, are billed and managed by Google
- cancellation, billing changes and refund requests are handled through the relevant store/platform policies

Do NOT create a custom MyLocalTrade refund policy for store subscriptions.

Do NOT imply MyLocalTrade directly processes App Store/Google Play subscription payments.


ISSUE 3 — TEAM / COMPANY PLAN WORDING

Inspect the current Terms and Privacy wording for Team / Company subscription features.

Make sure the legal text does not promise Team seats, employee access, or plan availability beyond what the app/store configuration currently supports.

The wording should describe Team functionality conditionally where appropriate, for example:

“Where a Team plan is available…”

Do not change Team billing/business logic in this task.


ISSUE 4 — ICO / PRIVACY REGISTRATION DATE

The audit found a statement using an ICO validity date of 21 July 2026, which is now outdated.

Please inspect the current Privacy Policy wording.

Do not invent or guess a new ICO expiry/validity date.

If the current valid date cannot be verified from the existing trusted project information:
- remove the time-sensitive expiry/validity date
- keep only accurate non-expiring wording such as the company being registered with the ICO, if that fact is already established in the project

Do not fabricate registration numbers, dates or legal status.


ISSUE 5 — CONSISTENCY ACROSS ALL LEGAL SURFACES

Please inspect all current MyLocalTrade legal surfaces, not just one screen:

- in-app Privacy Policy
- in-app Terms / Terms of Service
- registration/onboarding legal acceptance text
- subscription/billing legal notes
- phone verification consent/help text
- any web legal pages in this repository that are intended to represent the same policy

The same core facts should not contradict each other.

Do not rewrite unrelated sections unnecessarily.
