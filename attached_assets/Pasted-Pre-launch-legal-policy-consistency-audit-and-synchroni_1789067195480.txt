PRESERVE THESE CURRENT PRODUCT RULES
==================================================

Do not change these unless there is an actual contradiction:

APPLE / BILLING
- Current iOS launch uses Apple App Store in-app subscriptions.
- Cancellation/management is through the App Store.
- Refund wording must not imply MyLocalTrade directly controls Apple refunds.
- Do not add Google Play wording before Android launch.
- MyLocalTrade does not process payment for the underlying trader/customer job.

MODERATION
- Eligible moderation decisions can be appealed within 30 days of the decision.
- After the 30-day appeal window expires, the appeal option is unavailable.
- An appeal is a separate review and does not automatically reverse the original decision.
- Safety handling may continue independently.
- Do not expose CSEA/internal specialist workflow details publicly.

COMPLAINTS
- Keep the moderation appeal deadline separate from the general complaints process.
- Existing complaint acknowledgement and substantive-response commitments must remain clearly distinct.

AGE
- Service is intended for adults aged 18+.
- Keep current legal-capacity declaration consistent with registration.
- Do not invent DOB checks, biometric age checks or age-verification services that do not exist.

VERIFICATION
- Do not imply that verification guarantees quality, honesty, competence, safety or outcome.
- Use accurate wording describing the checks actually performed.

MESSAGING / OTP
- Phone/RCS/SMS verification is for account access, security and trust.
- Never describe OTP messaging as marketing.
- Do not reintroduce old inaccurate customer SMS/RCS claims.

MARKETPLACE
- MyLocalTrade is a marketplace/intermediary.
- Trader and customer arrange the job.
- MyLocalTrade does not process the underlying job payment.
- Do not accidentally describe traders as employees or agents of MyLocalTrade.

COOKIES / TRACKING
- Preserve the current finding that MyLocalTrade does not currently use non-essential analytics/advertising tracking on the public landing site.
- Preserve accurate hosting-cookie wording where applicable.
- Do not add a cookie banner unless the actual implementation now requires one.
