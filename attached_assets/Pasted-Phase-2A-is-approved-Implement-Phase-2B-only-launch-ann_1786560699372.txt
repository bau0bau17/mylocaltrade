`MARKETING_DAILY_SEND_CAP=200`

`TRANSACTIONAL_EMAIL_DAILY_RESERVE=100`

UNSUBSCRIBE AND SUPPRESSION

Every launch and marketing email must contain:

- MyLocalTrade identity
- Privacy Policy link
- Visible unsubscribe option
- Valid contact information
- Appropriate Brevo/List-Unsubscribe support

Implement a secure unsubscribe flow that works without login.

For a visible web unsubscribe link:

- GET displays the unsubscribe page but does not change consent, protecting against link scanners.
- Explicit POST confirms the unsubscribe.
- The token must be secure and non-guessable.
- Do not store or log raw tokens.
- Remove the token from browser history where applicable.
- Apply `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`.
- The action must be immediate and idempotent.

Use the supported Brevo one-click unsubscribe/list-unsubscribe mechanism where required for email-client compatibility, and synchronise the result back into the local suppression state.

Unsubscribe must prevent future launch/marketing campaigns but must not block:

- Password resets
- Account verification
- Security notifications
- Team invitations
- Other necessary transactional account emails

Process or synchronise:

- Unsubscribes
- Hard bounces
- Spam complaints
- Brevo email blocks

Do not repeatedly retry permanently blocked addresses.

Do not allow the public Early Access form to bypass a complaint, hard-bounce or administrative suppression.
